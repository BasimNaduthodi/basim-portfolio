About

I studied money.
Then I fell for interfaces.

Hi, I’m Basim — a UX/UI and product designer from Kerala. My B.Com is still my edge: I understand the balances, payments and cash flow behind the fintech screens I design.

For almost three years at Evoqins I took apps from the first user flow to developer handoff. I care about the unglamorous parts: a hierarchy that reads at a glance, a flow with one less step, components developers love to reuse.

  • Product thinking — problems, flows, priorities
  • UX/UI for mobile apps, web and dashboards
  • Design systems that scale
  • Prototyping & AI-assisted exploration
